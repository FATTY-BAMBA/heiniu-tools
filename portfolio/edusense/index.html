EduSense.AI

讓一堂課，成為可以重新探索的知識。Turn a lecture into knowledge you can revisit.

EduSense 聚焦將長篇課程錄影整理為可使用、可檢索、能追溯來源的學習資源，讓學員不必反覆在影片中尋找重要內容。EduSense is designed around a practical learning problem: turning long-form recorded lessons into structured materials that students can explore and return to, with answers linked back to source content.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

錄影保存了課程，卻不一定讓內容容易學習。Recording a class is not the same as making it learnable.

一段長課程可能包含多個主題、例子與關鍵解釋，但學員往往只記得大概位置。重新尋找、複習與自我測驗都需要額外時間。Long lessons contain concepts, examples and explanations across a single timeline. Finding a specific answer, revisiting a difficult section or preparing for a quiz can require searching through the recording all over again.

此案例的產品方向，不是只生成摘要，而是從課程內容建立可重用的學習入口。The product direction is broader than a one-off summary: it aims to create reusable entry points into the source material.

02 / EXPERIENCE MODEL

從課程錄影，到四種學習入口。One recording. Four learning entry points.

四種學習入口讓學生能按主題探索、複習、練習與查找課程知識。Four complementary study experiences help students navigate, review, practice and find course knowledge.

01 / STRUCTURE

智慧分章Chapters

把長篇內容依主題整理，幫助學員直接返回相關單元。Navigate directly to relevant topics instead of scrubbing through the entire video.

02 / REVIEW

課程筆記Study notes

將重要概念整理成可複習的文字資源。Revisit key concepts in a compact reading format.

03 / PRACTICE

測驗與題目Questions & quizzes

從教材形成練習題，支援主動複習。Practice understanding with questions based on lesson material.

04 / FIND

來源導向問答Source-aware Q&A

讓問答回到原始課程脈絡，並保留核對來源的能力。Ask about lesson material while maintaining a pathway back to the original source.

03 / ENGINEERING CONSIDERATIONS

真正困難的是把生成內容變成可信的學習體驗。The challenge is not just generating text. It is preserving learning context.

01

資料與來源關聯Source linkage

從影片產生的章節、筆記和回答，應保留通往原始課程的路徑；精確的來源呈現方式仍待實際介面驗證。Generated chapters, notes and answers should remain traceable to the lesson. The precise source-linking behavior must be confirmed in the actual interface.

02

處理階段Multi-stage processing

公開產品規格採用多階段處理架構，將影音輸入轉換成可探索的學習內容；各階段的具體實作以產品技術資料為準。The published product specification describes a multi-stage workflow for converting lecture media into navigable study content; the implementation details depend on the product version.

03

語言與使用者脈絡Language and learner context

公開產品介紹強調繁體中文與機構資料隔離。這些設計目標值得呈現，但仍需文件及測試佐證其部署範圍。Published product materials emphasize Traditional Chinese and per-institution data isolation. Their production boundaries still require deployment-specific evidence.

04 / EVIDENCE AND BOUNDARIES

功能範圍與評估邊界。Product scope and evaluation boundaries.

公開產品頁描述智慧分章、筆記、問答、學習分析等功能；但功能說明本身並非成效測試。此案例暫不聲稱準確率、使用者人數、處理速度或學習成效。Public product materials describe chaptering, notes, question answering and broader learning analytics. Marketing descriptions are not equivalent to measured outcomes, so this case study intentionally makes no unsupported claims about accuracy, users, speed or learning gains.

此頁以公開產品資料呈現設計方向與工程考量，並保留版本差異：詳細架構與量化成果以正式技術資料為準。This case study draws on public product information to explain the experience and engineering considerations. Detailed architecture and measured outcomes depend on version-specific technical evidence.

05 / NEXT EVIDENCE

怎麼評估這樣的學習工具？What would good learning outcomes require?

工程成果應從資料處理品質、章節與課程內容的對應、來源追溯，以及實際學員使用情境評估。Meaningful evaluation would assess content-processing quality, alignment between chapters and source lessons, provenance of answers, and performance in real learning tasks.
