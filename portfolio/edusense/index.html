EduSense.AI

讓一堂課，成為可以重新探索的知識。Turn a lecture into knowledge you can revisit.

EduSense 將長篇課程影片轉化為可探索的學習內容，並結合 AI 助教、學習互動分析和教師儀表板，連結學生的學習體驗與教學決策。EduSense turns recorded lessons into structured learning experiences and connects them with an AI teaching assistant, learning interaction insights, and educator-facing intelligence.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

錄影保存了課程，卻不一定讓內容容易學習。Recording a class is not the same as making it learnable.

一段長課程可能包含多個主題、例子與關鍵解釋，但學員往往只記得大概位置。重新尋找、複習與自我測驗都需要額外時間。Long lessons contain concepts, examples and explanations across a single timeline. Finding a specific answer, revisiting a difficult section or preparing for a quiz can require searching through the recording all over again.

此案例的產品方向，不是只生成摘要，而是從課程內容建立可重用的學習入口。The product direction is broader than a one-off summary: it aims to create reusable entry points into the source material.

02 / EXPERIENCE MODEL

一堂錄影課，變成完整的學習體驗。One recorded class. An entire learning experience.

四種學習入口讓學生能按主題探索、複習、練習與查找課程知識。Four complementary study experiences help students navigate, review, practice and find course knowledge.

01 / STRUCTURE

智慧分章Chapters

把長篇內容依主題整理，幫助學員直接返回相關單元。Navigate directly to relevant topics instead of scrubbing through the entire video.

02 / REVIEW

課程筆記Study notes

將重要概念整理成可複習的文字資源。Revisit key concepts in a compact reading format.

03 / PRACTICE

測驗與題目Questions & quizzes

從教材形成練習題，支援主動複習。Practice understanding with questions based on lesson material.

04 / FIND

來源導向問答Source-aware Q&A

讓問答回到原始課程脈絡，並保留核對來源的能力。Ask about lesson material while maintaining a pathway back to the original source.

03 / EDUCATOR INTELLIGENCE

不只回答學員的問題，也讓教師看懂學習狀況。Beyond answering questions: make learning patterns visible.

EduSense 的學習分析產品涵蓋學員支持分級、全班知識缺口，以及提前發現流失風險的訊號。EduSense extends beyond content generation with student-support classification, class-wide knowledge-gap insights, and early dropout-risk signals.

01 / STUDENT SUPPORT

學員狀況分級Student classification

依學習互動辨識表現良好、需要關注及急需協助的學員，讓教師更容易安排個別支持。Classify learners by support needs—from progressing well to needing additional or urgent attention—so educators can prioritize follow-up.

02 / CLASS INSIGHT

知識缺口儀表板Knowledge-gap dashboard

觀察未掌握的重點與反覆提問的主題，協助教師調整課程講解與複習安排。Surface concepts learners struggle with and topics repeatedly raised in questions, helping teachers adapt explanations and review plans.

03 / EARLY ATTENTION

流失風險訊號Dropout-risk signals

結合登入頻率與互動深度等訊號，協助教育團隊提早發現需要關懷的學員。Use engagement signals such as login frequency and interaction depth to identify students who may need timely support.

04 / PLATFORM ENGINEERING

把影音理解、可追溯問答與學習洞察整合為一個平台。Unify video understanding, grounded assistance, and learning intelligence.

01

資料與來源關聯Source linkage

AI 助教依課堂內容提供回答與影片時間點，讓學生能回到老師原本的講解核對知識。The teaching-assistant experience connects answers to the teacher’s lesson content and video timestamps, giving learners a path back to the explanation.

02

處理階段Multi-stage processing

EduSense 的公開產品規格採用九階段內容處理管線，將課程錄影整理為結構化學習資源。The published EduSense architecture specifies a nine-stage processing pipeline for transforming recorded lessons into structured learning resources.

03

語言與使用者脈絡Language and learner context

產品面向繁體中文教學環境，並將不同教育機構的資料分開處理，支援更有脈絡的學習互動。The platform is tailored to Traditional Chinese learning environments, with institution-level data separation and context-aware educational interactions.

05 / PRODUCT VALUE

讓內容產生、學員互動與教學決策彼此連結。Connect content, student engagement, and teaching decisions.

學員可依主題複習課程、練習問題並向 AI 助教提問；教師則可透過學習互動了解哪些主題需要重新解釋，哪些學員值得主動關懷。Students can navigate lessons, practice questions, and ask the AI teaching assistant for help. Educators can use interaction insights to identify difficult topics and prioritize student support.

從「課堂講了什麼」到「學員理解了多少」，EduSense 把教學內容與可採取行動的學習洞察放在同一個產品體驗中。From “what was taught” to “where learners need help,” EduSense brings lesson content and actionable learning insights into one connected product experience.

06 / EVALUATION

怎麼評估這樣的學習工具？What would good learning outcomes require?

工程成果應從資料處理品質、章節與課程內容的對應、來源追溯，以及實際學員使用情境評估。Meaningful evaluation would assess content-processing quality, alignment between chapters and source lessons, provenance of answers, and performance in real learning tasks.
