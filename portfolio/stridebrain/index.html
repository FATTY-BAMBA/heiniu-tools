StrideBrain.

專案型企業的營運智慧層。The operating intelligence layer for project-based businesses.

StrideBrain 把目前專案、公司知識、歷史經驗與工作流程連結成一個不斷累積智慧的營運環境。團隊能更快找到資訊、理解風險、做出有依據的決策，並逐步將重複工作交由受控 AI 代理協助。StrideBrain connects current projects, company knowledge, historical experience and operating workflows into an intelligence layer that compounds over time. Teams can find what the company knows, understand live work, make evidence-backed decisions, and increasingly delegate repeatable tasks to governed AI agents.

01 / THE PROBLEM

企業的工作現況與知識，不該散落在不同工具裡。Company intelligence should not live in disconnected silos.

公司的日常運作跨越目前專案、文件、供應商、報價、風險、決策與同事的經驗。資料一旦被分散，團隊不只難以借鑑過去，也難以看清眼前最重要的工作。Everyday operations span active projects, documents, suppliers, quotations, risks, decisions and team expertise. When context is fragmented, people struggle not only to learn from the past but also to understand what needs attention now.

StrideBrain 讓這些資訊成為互相關聯的營運記憶與工作環境。相似歷史專案分析是第一個深入實作的垂直場景，而不是整個平台。StrideBrain turns that context into connected operating memory and an intelligent workplace. Comparable Project Intelligence is the first deep vertical slice of a broader platform—not the whole product.

四種能力，連成一個持續學習的系統。Four connected capabilities. One compounding operating layer.

03 / V0.1 CONTROLLED DEMO

第一個聚焦場景：相似歷史專案分析。First focused slice: Comparable Project Intelligence.

01 / CONTEXT

確認目前專案Start in context

在當前專案及報價版本中開始，不再手動重述全部背景。Work from the active project and its current quotation.

02 / COMPARISON

找出可比較的歷史專案Compare history

依結構化條件排序並說明相似點與關鍵差異。Rank eligible historical projects with explainable similarities and differences.

03 / EVIDENCE

追溯支持判斷的資料Inspect evidence

區分報價、預估成本、供應商報價和核對後的實際成本。Keep quoted prices, forecasts, supplier offers and reconciled actuals distinct.

04 / REVIEW

建立內部審核行動Create a review action

在權限確認後產生有限的內部審核事項，而非自動執行外部或財務動作。Propose a bounded internal review with authorization, not automatic external sending or financial approval.

此流程以 v0.1 的受控示範範圍為依據，從比較歷史專案，到查閱證據並建立內部審核。This workflow follows the v0.1 controlled-demo scope, from historical comparison and evidence inspection to internal review.

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

為什麼不是另一個企業聊天機器人？Why not just another enterprise chatbot?

01

計算與解釋分開Deterministic comparisons

v0.1 的相似度和成本比較由可版本化的結構化規則計算，模型文字說明不能改變排序或權威數值。The v0.1 comparison specification uses versioned, structured policies for similarity and economics. Optional model prose must not override ranking or authoritative values.

02

來源的權威性比答案重要Evidence and authority

報價不等於實際成本。過期、衝突、不足或權限受限的資料必須被辨別，必要時拒絕產出不可靠的範圍。A quotation is not an actual cost. Stale, conflicted, insufficient or unauthorized records must be distinguished; the system abstains when evidence is not good enough.

03

行動必須經過授權Governed actions

第一階段僅允許經確認的內部審核行動，權限由伺服器判定，並保留稽核紀錄。The first slice limits writes to confirmed internal review actions. Authorization is server-derived and actions produce an audit trail.

04

示範資料不是客戶真實資料A controlled demo boundary

產品決策文件明確使用虛構組織與合成資料來測試流程；受控示範不應被描述成已使用真實客戶資料的全面正式上線。The product contract uses a fictional organization and synthetic fixtures. A controlled demonstration must not be described as a production rollout with real customer data.

04 / STATUS AND EVIDENCE

證據必須走在動作之前。Evidence before action.

先有證據，再採取行動。StrideBrain 的比較與建議以授權的公司資料為依據，讓重要來源可供檢視；涉及實際業務變更的動作必須通過權限檢查與適當的人工作業確認。Evidence before action. StrideBrain's comparison design grounds recommendations in authorized company knowledge, makes important sources inspectable, and requires permission checks and appropriate human review before consequential business actions.

v0.1 技術架構包含可解釋的專案相似度、報價與實際成本的權威性區分、可互動的證據檢視，以及權限控管與審核記錄，形成由比較到決策的完整工程流程。The v0.1 architecture brings together explainable project similarity, clearly separated quotation and actual-cost authority, interactive evidence inspection, and permission-aware review with an audit trail—a coherent engineering path from comparison to decision.

05 / NEXT EVIDENCE

怎樣判斷一個 AI 工作流值得信任？What makes an AI workflow trustworthy?

有價值的評估包括歷史專案排名是否合理、來源是否可追溯、財務資訊是否恰當遮蔽，以及內部審核是否受到權限保護。The important validation questions are whether comparisons rank relevant history, evidence can be inspected, sensitive economics remain permission-safe, and internal review actions honor authorization.

VALIDATION: 排名一致性 · 證據來源 · 權限隔離 · 審核追蹤Ranking consistency · Evidence · Access control · Audit trail
