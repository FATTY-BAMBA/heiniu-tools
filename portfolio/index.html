COMPUTER VISION

從空拍影像分割建物Mapping buildings from aerial imagery

在高密度市區中，緊鄰的屋頂與不規則輪廓容易被合併成同一塊。Closely spaced roofs and irregular footprints make individual buildings difficult to distinguish in dense urban imagery.

方法Method
我們結合 Mask R-CNN、PointRend、遷移學習與資料增強，生成個別建物的遮罩。We combined Mask R-CNN, PointRend, transfer learning, and data augmentation to produce masks for individual buildings.
研究結果Finding
論文展示方法能自動擷取研究區內形狀各異的建物。資料及程式碼可依論文說明向通訊作者申請。The study demonstrated automatic extraction of distinct, complex-shaped buildings in the study area. The paper provides a request pathway for data and code.

MOBILE MACHINE LEARNING

運用 AI 行動應用程式進行邊坡災害防減Slope Disaster Mitigation Using AI-Based Mobile Applications

已發表的研究介紹團隊開發的兩款循環神經網路 iOS 應用程式，分別用於評估地震作用與孔隙水壓變化下的岩坡安全。專利與目前下載狀態尚待核實。Our published research describes two RNN-based iOS applications for rock slope assessment under earthquake loading and pore-water pressure changes. Patent status and current distribution are not yet independently verified.

方法Method
研究團隊以循環神經網路建立兩款 iOS 應用程式，分別處理地震與孔隙水壓對岩坡穩定性的影響。We developed two iOS applications using recurrent neural networks. One models the effect of earthquakes and the other models pore-water pressure.
研究結果Finding
在三個未納入訓練的地震案例中，模型預測的穩定數與數值分析結果相比，誤差為 2.08% 至 4.84%（論文表 VI）。這些應用程式用於初步篩查，現場安全決策仍需專業評估。Across three earthquake cases outside training, the predicted stability numbers differed from numerical-analysis results by 2.08% to 4.84% (paper, Table VI). The applications support preliminary screening. Site safety decisions require professional assessment.
