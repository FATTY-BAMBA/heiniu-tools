讓相鄰建物，保有各自的邊界。One city block. Individual buildings.

在密集市區的空拍影像中，建物屋頂可能緊密相連，形狀也各不相同。這篇共同研究探討如何自動定位並為每棟建物產生獨立遮罩。In dense urban aerial imagery, neighboring rooftops sit close together and vary in shape. This coauthored study investigates how to locate and segment each building as a separate instance.

01 / THE PROBLEM

偵測到「建物」還不夠。Finding buildings isn't enough.

一般的語義分割可以辨認影像中的建築區域，但對於緊鄰的屋頂，仍可能難以分開每棟建物。這項研究把問題設定為實例分割：除了識別建物，還要區分各個建物個體。Semantic segmentation can label building pixels, but it does not inherently provide one separate mask for each touching rooftop. The study frames the task as instance segmentation: detect building objects and assign distinct masks to individual buildings.

這在高密度市區特別重要，因為不規則輪廓與緊密排列會增加自動提取建物的難度。Irregular roof contours and small gaps in dense urban areas make accurate footprint extraction especially challenging.

02 / REPORTED METHOD

從多光譜影像到獨立建物遮罩。From multispectral input to individual masks.

01 / INPUT

多光譜空拍資料Multispectral aerial imagery

處理高密度市區的高解析度建物影像。Input imagery captures closely spaced urban buildings.

02 / DATA

遷移學習與資料增強Transfer learning + augmentation

利用既有模型知識與增強策略來擴充訓練資料。Use pretrained model knowledge and augmentation strategies to broaden training examples.

03 / INSTANCE MODEL

Mask R-CNN + PointRend

結合實例定位與細緻遮罩預測。Combine instance localization with finer mask prediction.

04 / OUTPUT

個別建物遮罩Building-instance masks

目標是分別呈現輪廓不同且相鄰的建物。Produce distinct masks for buildings with complex, neighboring footprints.

此為論文摘要描述的方法流程摘要，非原始模型執行追蹤。A method overview based on the published abstract, not a live execution trace.

03 / PUBLISHED FINDING

論文展示了什麼？What does the paper establish?

作者報告，所提出的實例分割方法能夠自動提取研究區內形狀獨特且複雜的建物。這是論文報告的研究結果，不代表本頁已獨立重現模型或取得完整測試資料。The authors report that the proposed instance-segmentation method successfully extracts distinctive and complex-shaped buildings in the studied dense urban area. This is the published finding—not an independent reproduction or a claim of production deployment.
